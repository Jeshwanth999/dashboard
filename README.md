##Inside the workbook:

Orders — raw transactional sales data
Returns — returned order records
People — regional manager mapping
KPI / report — calculated summary metrics feeding the dashboard
Dashboard sheet — the final interactive visual layer

## how to use

Download the excel sheet
Open in Microsoft Excel (Excel 2016+ recommended for full slicer/pivot support)
Use the slicers on each panel to filter by Category, Region, or Time Period
All KPI cards and charts update automatically based on your selection

##💡 Key Insights Uncovered

-Sales show a strong upward trend toward year-end, peaking around months 10–12
-East and West regions contribute the largest share of total revenue
-Standard Class is the dominant shipping mode by order volume
-A concentrated set of top 10 products and customers account for a disproportionate share of total sales — a classic -80/20 pattern worth deeper investigation
