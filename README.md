##Inside the workbook:<br>

Orders — raw transactional sales data<br>
Returns — returned order records<br>
People — regional manager mapping<br>
KPI / report — calculated summary metrics feeding the dashboard<br>
Dashboard sheet — the final interactive visual layer<br>

## how to use<br>

Download the excel sheet<br>
Open in Microsoft Excel (Excel 2016+ recommended for full slicer/pivot support)<br>
Use the slicers on each panel to filter by Category, Region, or Time Period<br>
All KPI cards and charts update automatically based on your selection<br>

##💡 Key Insights Uncovered<br>

-Sales show a strong upward trend toward year-end, peaking around months 10–12<br>
-East and West regions contribute the largest share of total revenue<br>
-Standard Class is the dominant shipping mode by order volume<br>
-A concentrated set of top 10 products and customers account for a disproportionate share of total sales — a classic -80/20 pattern worth deeper investigation<br>
